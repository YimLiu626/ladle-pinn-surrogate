Figure 5: Ablation and baseline comparison. (a) Physics term ablation. Dashed line marks the pure-data reference (R-squared = 0.859). Continuity constraint provides the only measurable improvement (+0.014 R-squared). (b) Baseline comparison. POD-ROM achieves the highest center-blowing accuracy (R-squared = 0.994); MLP 8x384 follows at 0.983; DeepONet fails (R-squared = -0.72).

Figure 6: Cross-position generalization. PINN center-only vs. PINN joint (m in {0,1}) vs. POD center-only. Center-only models collapse on eccentric data; joint PINN recovers R-squared = 0.94-0.97; POD fails catastrophically (R-squared < 0).

Figure 7: POD cross-position failure vs. PINN generalization at Q=120 NL/min. (a) POD trained on center data, tested on center: R-squared = 0.994. (b) Same POD model tested on eccentric data: R-squared = -1.40. (c) Joint PINN on eccentric data: R-squared = 0.969. Bottom panel: error distribution histogram comparing POD center Q120, POD blind eccentric Q120, and PINN eccentric Q120.

Figure 8: Convergence analysis for the 6x128 MLP. (a) Training loss (MSE, log scale). (b) R-squared evolution for pure-data and continuity-regularized training. (c) R-squared at key checkpoints (500, 1000, 1500, 2000 epochs).

Figure 9: Performance summary and sensitivity. (a) Per-condition R-squared for the 8x384 MLP (training vs. extrapolation). (b) Multi-seed ensemble analysis: R-squared = 0.978 +/- 0.001 for the joint 8x384 model. (c) VOF strategy ablation: soft filtering (alpha_steel > 0.01) outperforms hard thresholding (alpha_steel > 0.5) by +0.344 R-squared.
